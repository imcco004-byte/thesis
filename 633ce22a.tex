\begin{abstract}

In this paper we measure the expected lifetime of Barium[+] D5/2. Barium ions are trapped in a quadrapole ion trap and are excited to the P3/2 with lasers. We record the time of emission from the metastable D5/2 state to the P1/2 under different temperatures.

\end{abstract}
