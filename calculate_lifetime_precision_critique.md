# Hegelian critique of calculate_lifetime_precision.py

- Master claim. The prior rewrite elevated explicit physical inputs as sovereign: every systematic had to enter from outside as a paper-style input.
- Slave labor hidden from it. The pipeline already does the concrete labor of judging frame credibility through matched SNR, measurement precision score, decision margin, undefined-margin fraction, off-modal fraction, and related diagnostics. Those are not external guesses; they are the detector's own confidence signals formed under noise and ambiguity.
- Non-essential negative object. Expelling manifest-level proxy heuristics was the right move, but the rewrite then negated all internal diagnostics with the same gesture and collapsed SNR-grounded evidence into the same category as unsupported external physics guesses.
- Contraposition failure. If undefined-margin fraction is near zero and weakest SNR remains cleanly above threshold, the detection-error and timing-ambiguity budgets should contract on their own. A pure explicit-input model cannot express that and therefore treats materially different data qualities as equivalent.
- Concrete truth. Three components are internally measurable from the camera diagnostics and should default from them, with CLI flags acting as overrides: detection_error_bound, ion_heating_false_dark, and timing_window_bias uncertainty. Three remain properly external and should not be faked from SNR: collision_quenching, blackbody_radiation, and leakage_light_deshelving.
- Public criterion. Every emitted systematic row should carry an origin tag such as snr_derived, cli_override, or explicit_physical_input, and the generating statistics should remain visible under source_statistics so the budget stays auditable rather than merely trusted.
